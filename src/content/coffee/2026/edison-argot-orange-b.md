Really interesting that this is the second time I have had these beers from Skylark this year. This batch is much more subdued, the aperol spritz orange is muted, the cup has changed little over the few weeks open in contrast to the first batch which transitioned from bright to mellow significantly. Whilst I prefer the punch and excitement of the first batch with its fruit forward aromas and bright cup, this second batch is actually a better and more balanced coffee.
